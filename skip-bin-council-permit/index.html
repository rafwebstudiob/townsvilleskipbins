Skip bin permits in Townsville: Townsville City Council rules

In short: What Townsville City Council requires, how to apply, and where to place a skip on your own property.

A skip on your own driveway usually doesn't need council approval. A skip on the road, footpath or nature strip is different. Here's what Townsville City Council says and what to check before you book.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short answer

On your own land, generally no. In the road reserve, such as the road or footpath, yes: Townsville City Council requires an approval for works on public areas and roads.

On your own property

Your own driveway, yard or a firm patch of ground is the easiest place for a skip. Council's approval is for skips in the road reserve, such as the road or footpath, so a bin wholly on your property generally doesn't need it. In the wet season, lawns get soft, so a driveway or hardstand is safer for the truck.

On the road, footpath or nature strip

Townsville City Council requires an approval for works on public areas and roads for activities in the road reserve, which covers placing a skip on a footpath or road. Council's planning team handles work zones and other works in the road reserve. Council doesn't publish a single skip fee online, so ask what applies to your street.

Before you book a skip on the street, check with Townsville City Council on 13 48 10, or the planning team on 07 4773 8303 or planning.assets@townsville.qld.gov.au. Mention it on the quote form too, because the operator may be able to advise.

What to ask council before you book

  1. Does my spot need an approval for works on public areas and roads, and should I or the skip operator apply?
  2. What does it cost this financial year, and how long does assessment take?
  3. How long can the skip stay, and are there conditions such as lights, reflectors or a set distance from the kerb?
  4. Are there extra rules for a busy road, a bus stop or a heritage area?

Ask for the answers in writing or note the date and who you spoke to.

Placing the skip safely

Keep the skip clear of driveways, hydrants, bus stops and drains, and don't fill above the rim. Before a storm, make sure loose items in the skip are weighed down or removed.

Where this information comes from

  • ABLIS: Approval for works on public areas and roads (Townsville City Council)
  • Townsville City Council planning: works in the road reserve (work zones, scaffolding and gantries)

Council rules, hours and fees change. Check with the council or facility before you rely on them.
